02 — Work With Me

Why collab with 1nijel

For brands, promoters, tourism boards and fellow creators who want reach that feels like word of mouth, not an ad.

  • High-engagement street host

    I walk up to strangers and get real reactions on camera. Those moments get shared, stitched and argued about in the comments, so your message travels further than a polished spot would.

  • Authentic grassroots connection

    I'm part of the communities I film: matchday crowds, Caribbean diaspora, carnival. People trust the content because they recognise themselves in it.

  • Technical production quality

    I'm an engineer by day, and I bring that discipline to the shoot: clean audio, sharp edits, fast turnaround, and deliverables cut for every platform.

Got a brief?

Sponsored segments, event coverage, brand activations and creator crossovers.
